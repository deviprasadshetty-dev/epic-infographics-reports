01 — Executive Overview

Dual-Region Startup Ecosystem Mapping

Google structures its startup initiatives through regional leads paired with global accelerator directors. Access points fall into three main functional pillars: Regional Founder Programs, Accelerator Cohorts, and Google Cloud / DeepMind AI Infrastructure.

  • India Lead (Screenshot Match): Ragini Das serves as Head of Google for Startups - India, spearheading country-wide founder strategy and community growth alongside Accelerator Lead Farish CV.
  • United States & North America Lead: Danny Navarro serves as Head of Google for Startups - North America, managing US startup programs, regional hubs, and founder funds.
  • Global Accelerator & AI Gatekeepers: Kevin O'Toole heads Google's global accelerator network, while Matt Ridenour directs startup partnerships specifically for Google DeepMind & AI Labs (Gemini / AI Studio).
  • Cloud Compute & Credits ($350K Scale): Scott Barneson (MD, North America Startups at Google Cloud) and Kathryn Van Nuys (Global Director, VC & PE Partnerships) manage institutional tier infrastructure allocations in the US, mirrored by Karan Kumar and Nupur Verma in India.
