01 — Executive Overview

Target Ecosystem & Context

Google for Startups in India operates across three complementary units: Strategic Ecosystem Initiatives, the dedicated Accelerator (AI First Cohorts), and Google Cloud Startup Programs.

  • Primary Target Identified: The profile in reference is Ragini Das, appointed as Head of Google for Startups - India (also co-founder of leap.club), directing macro founder strategy, ecosystem equity, and high-level partnerships.
  • Cohort Gatekeepers: Startups seeking accelerator entry or direct cohort mentorship report into Farish CV (Regional Lead, AI Startup Programs) and Paul Ravindranath G (Regional Lead, Developer Relations - Startup Programs).
  • Venture & Cloud Capital Gatekeepers: Founders targeting cloud infrastructure scale, credits ($200k-$350k USD tier), and VC connections interface with Nupur Verma (Lead - AI Partnerships with VC/Govt) and Karan Kumar (Lead - VC & Startups, Google Cloud).

Response Rate Benchmark
  • LinkedIn InMail: Highest conversion (65-75%)

The proven route to securing a conversation is a verified LinkedIn connection combined with a referral or direct application through Google for Startups portal submissions.
