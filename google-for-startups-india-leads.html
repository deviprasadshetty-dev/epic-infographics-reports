01 — Ecosystem Architecture

Complete Chain of Command

Google for Startups operates as a coordinated multi-tier organization spanning Alphabet, Google Cloud, and Google DeepMind. The organization runs under unified global leadership with autonomous regional leads in North America and India.

  • Global Vice President: Darren Mowry serves as VP of Global Startups at Google, holding ultimate P&L and strategic authority over all worldwide startup, venture, and cloud founder initiatives.
  • Global Accelerator Gatekeeper: Kevin O'Toole (Global Head of Google Accelerators) and Karen Fiester (Global Director) oversee all accelerator cohorts worldwide.
  • India Leadership (Screenshot Match): Ragini Das leads overall strategy as Head of Google for Startups - India, while Farish CV controls the flagship Google for Startups Accelerator: India (AI First) cohort.
  • US & North America Leadership: Danny Navarro leads North America operations, Scott Barneson directs North America startup cloud infrastructure, and Matt Ridenour directs startup partnerships for Google DeepMind & Google Labs (Gemini API & AI Studio).
  • Venture & Non-Dilutive Capital: Samantha Rodriguez manages US VC partnerships and the Google Founders Funds, while Kathryn Van Nuys leads global VC & PE relationships across Tier-1 funds.
